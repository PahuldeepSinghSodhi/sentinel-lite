# Reviewer task study (protocol, not results)

Sentinel-Lite does not yet claim measured manual-review time savings. To test that claim, prepare a small set of cases with answer keys for anomaly type, relevant source row or page, and correct reviewer disposition. Include at least a rate overcharge, a rate-card exception, a duplicate, an irrelevant contract clause, and a case with no contract.

Recruit reviewers who have not seen the answer key. Have each reviewer complete comparable cases in two conditions: source files alone, and Sentinel-Lite with its scan and optional investigation brief. Alternate which condition comes first across reviewers to reduce learning/order effects. Start timing when the reviewer receives the case, and stop when they submit a decision with cited evidence. Record elapsed time **and** decision correctness, missing evidence, and any misleading suggested clause. Treat an incorrect but fast decision as a failure, not a time saving.

Report the number of reviewers and cases, per-condition medians and ranges, paired time differences, and correctness. Keep the raw, de-identified measurements and task definitions. Do not extrapolate from this convenience sample to arbitrary company contracts; use a larger independent study before a strong productivity claim.
